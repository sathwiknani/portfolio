About Me

I am Yeshwanth Reddy Patlolla, currently pursuing a Doctor of Philosophy in Biological Sciences at Michigan Technological University. My research journey spans both India and the United States, rooted in a deep passion for plant science, sustainable agriculture, and food security.

As a Graduate Research Fellow at ICRISAT — the world-leading dryland crops research institute — I conducted landmark research on the longevity of Arachis (groundnut) seeds under varying temperature and moisture conditions. Working with ICRISAT's premier gene bank, I contributed to hybridization of pigeon peas and pioneering soil analysis methodologies.

At Murray State University, I managed full-scale farm operations including planting, harvesting, equipment maintenance, and staff supervision — collaborating closely with academic researchers and extension agents to apply agro-technology and crop rotation strategies for maximum productivity.

Currently at KWS Group, a global leader in plant breeding, I am contributing to crop improvement research focused on developing high-yielding, climate-resilient wheat varieties.

"My goal is to apply knowledge and skills in practical ways — helping farmers increase their economic feasibility and sustainability. I believe technology and innovation can transform the agriculture industry and benefit the country's GDP."
